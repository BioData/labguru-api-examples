How to use this page
  • Search for what you need, or click a table name to browse its fields.
  • Click Copy next to a field.
  • In Labguru, click into your form and paste (⌘V or Ctrl+V). Done.
  • Need several fields from the same table? Use Copy all at the top of that table to grab them in one go.
  • The short descriptions are our best guess at what each field means — a few unusual ones are marked "(not confirmed)". Spot anything wrong? Let us know.
